# Job offer collection

The application collects offers shown on selected Just Join IT and No Fluff Jobs listing pages.

## Language

**Listing**:
A filtered job site page that identifies a set of job offers.
_Avoid_: Search, category

**Offer**:
A single job posting linked from a listing. The same offer may occur on multiple listings.
_Avoid_: Listing item

**Candidate profile**:
The candidate's career evidence derived from their CV.
_Avoid_: CV copy

**Match assessment**:
An evaluation of an offer's technical requirements and role level against the candidate profile, including transferable skills. Its ordered levels run from no fit to very good fit and prioritize offers for manual review.
_Avoid_: Hiring prediction

**Shortlist**:
Offers whose match assessments meet the chosen threshold and await manual review.
_Avoid_: Applications
